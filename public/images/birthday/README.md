# 📸 Photos Folder: `public/images/birthday/`

Welcome! This folder is where you can place the real photos of the birthday girl.

## How to add your photos:

1. Copy your photos into this folder:
   - `photo-01.jpg`
   - `photo-02.jpg`
   - `photo-03.jpg`
   - `photo-04.jpg`
   - `photo-05.jpg`
   - `photo-06.jpg`
   - `photo-07.jpg`
   - `photo-08.jpg`
   - `photo-09.jpg`
   - `photo-10.jpg`

2. Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` (you can also use any filename and update `src/config/birthdayConfig.ts`).

3. Customize captions & dates:
   Open `src/config/birthdayConfig.ts` to customize the captions, tags, and dates for each photograph.
